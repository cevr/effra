# Restricted ordinary actor receive loops

Deferred Unit3 of [actors](../../specs/actors.md), independent from handler conformance. First demonstrate a guarantee beyond handlers with pacing, through two unrelated ordinary loop consumers carrying state or selecting timed/other sources. A general cancellation-safe handleNext is interruptible before dequeue and completes actor-owned dispatch/cleanup/reply once dequeued; no lost admitted message. Initial loops require empty raises; constructor/capture requirements remain visible. Ordinary loops beyond current support require general checked control flow, never actor-only passthrough. Raw responder escape/double completion, split completion, multiple consumers, batching and selective receive remain unsupported until general affine/correlation evidence exists. No new receive keyword justified by a wrapper. Keep deferred if the distinct benefit is unproved; otherwise its own both-target controls, full gate and independent review precede closure.
